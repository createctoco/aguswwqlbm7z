This Privacy Policy explains how OUOOO handles information when you browse ouooo.com or contact us about products, wholesale sourcing, customization, or support.

## Information you choose to provide

OUOOO does not currently operate customer accounts, a shopping cart, or online payment collection. We receive information when you contact us through WhatsApp, WeChat, or email. This may include:

- Your name, company, country, and contact details.
- Product references, quantities, target market, and delivery destination.
- Customization, packaging, artwork, and private-label requirements.
- Messages, files, and other information you include in the conversation.

Please do not send payment card details, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information through these channels.

## Technical information

When you visit the website, hosting and security providers may process standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested page, time of access, referring page, and security events. This information is used to deliver the static website, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and maintain performance and security.

OUOOO currently does not use advertising trackers or provide account-based personalization. Essential security or network cookies may be set by infrastructure providers where required.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Reply to inquiries and prepare quotations.
- Confirm specifications, samples, customization, production, and shipping requirements.
- Provide customer service and resolve quality or delivery concerns.
- Improve product information, website usability, security, and reliability.
- Meet legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution obligations where applicable.

## Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with:

- Manufacturing, sourcing, quality-control, packaging, and logistics partners involved in your request.
- Website hosting, security, email, and communications providers.
- Professional advisers or authorities where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect legitimate rights.

WhatsApp, WeChat, email providers, and other external services process data under their own privacy terms. Please review those services before using them.

## International processing and retention

OUOOO serves global buyers, so information may be processed in countries different from your own. We keep inquiry and business records only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a sourcing relationship, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. Use the contact channel most appropriate for the sensitivity of your information.

## Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete information you provided, subject to legal and legitimate business retention requirements. You may also ask us to stop non-essential follow-up communications.

## Children's privacy

OUOOO is a B2B sourcing website intended for professional buyers and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

## Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website, contact methods, or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will be published on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email [intl@ouooo.com](mailto:intl@ouooo.com) or use our [contact page](/contact).
