      were designed to be held in the palm, kept in a pocket, or tucked inside a Bible for quiet, personal prayer — not
      displayed as large objects.
    </GuideInfoCard>
    <GuideInfoCard eyebrow="Church pieces" title="10 mm belongs to display">
      In European tradition, 10 mm beads mostly appear as church ornaments, altar pieces, or wall-hanging rosaries.
      Ordinary believers rarely use this size for reciting the Rosary, which is why large-bead rosaries read as decor
      rather than prayer tools.
    </GuideInfoCard>
