OUOOO supports custom sourcing for wholesale rosaries, Catholic gifts, devotional jewelry, ornaments, and related packaging. Feasibility and minimum order quantity depend on the product, material, process, and design.

## What can be customized

- **Materials:** wood, glass, acrylic, plastic, natural stone, metal, cord, and mixed materials where suitable.
- **Color and size:** bead color, bead diameter, cord color, plating, and coordinated color combinations.
- **Religious components:** crucifix, cross, centerpiece, medal, saint image, charm, and related findings.
- **Construction:** knot style, spacing, connectors, chain, elastic, and assembly method.
- **Branding:** logo, parish or church name, laser marking, printing, insert cards, labels, and hangtags, subject to the selected material and process.
- **Packaging:** OPP bags, pouches, gift boxes, color boxes, display cards, barcodes, and private-label information.

## How a custom project works

1. **Send a brief.** Provide a product link or reference, target quantity, destination, preferred material, colors, dimensions, packaging, and artwork.
2. **Feasibility review.** We review construction, tooling, compliance, MOQ, sample requirements, and an estimated schedule.
3. **Quotation.** You receive the proposed specification, price basis, sample or tooling charges, production lead time, and shipping assumptions.
4. **Sample approval.** Where sampling is required, bulk production starts only after the sample and written specification are approved.
5. **Production and inspection.** Production follows the approved reference. Inspection evidence or third-party inspection can be discussed before shipment.

## What to include in your brief

- Clear reference photos from several angles.
- Editable artwork or a high-resolution logo where branding is required.
- Target dimensions, material, color references, and acceptable tolerances.
- Estimated order quantity and repeat-order expectation.
- Packaging format, barcode, labeling, and destination-market requirements.
- Delivery country and target date.

## Important custom-order notes

Customized and made-to-order products are produced for one buyer and usually cannot be resold. After the approved sample or specification enters production, changes may create additional cost or delay. Reasonable natural-material, handcrafted, and batch variations should be agreed during sampling.

Custom orders are generally not returnable for change of mind. If delivered goods materially differ from the approved sample or written specification, contact us promptly so the issue can be reviewed under our [return policy](/returns).
